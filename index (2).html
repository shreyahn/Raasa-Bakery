The Story Behind Raasa

Born from Passion, Made with Love

Raasa Bakery was founded by Surabhi H N, a self-taught baker and BMS College of Engineering graduate who turned her lifelong love for baking into a beautiful creative venture. In Sanskrit, Raasa means "essence" — the most vital and enjoyable part of something. Every cake, cheesecake, and cupcake we make carries that essence.

We specialize in vegetarian, eggless desserts crafted with the finest ingredients, designed to celebrate life's small, meaningful moments.
